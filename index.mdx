## The business in one paragraph

WeekendAI Labs is a four-brand applied-AI company: an education arm (**School of Applied AI**), an agency (**Studios by WeekendAI**), a hub (**weekendai.com**) and a publishing and community arm (**The AI Edge**). The brands share one audience and feed one super-community. The engine is problem-first AI education for non-technical professionals. The moat is the community. The goal is a business that makes money without depending on the founder, and that a buyer could run without him.

## New since the investor deck: CapabilityOS

The School of Applied AI also runs **[CapabilityOS](/brands/capabilityos)** ([capos.live](https://capos.live)): AI in 10 minutes a day, delivered by chat, on a yearly plan at $650 ($54/month). It adds about **$2.6M** to Year-3 revenue in the model (**$17.2M** total against the deck's $14.6M) and lifts recurring revenue to about 51%.

## What the operating model tells us

We rebuilt the revenue path line by line in the [operating model](/financials/three-year-plan). The volumes in the deck do add up to the deck's revenue. The model also shows three things to manage:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1. Recurring share is still short of the 55% sale-readiness gate" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Recurring and contracted revenue reaches about **51%** in Year 3 with CapabilityOS subscriptions (45% without), not 55%. Closing the rest takes roughly $0.75M of revenue moving onto recurring terms. The levers are: moving Academy buyers onto CapabilityOS annual plans, multi-year B2B terms, and annual Guild billing. See [Unit economics](/business-model/unit-economics#closing-the-recurring-gap).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2. Year-1 CAC runs above target" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Blended CAC is about **$233** in Year 1 against a target below $180. It reaches target from Year 2, once organic and referral traffic from the community carries more of the acquisition load. The free monthly live workshop and the free 7-day CapabilityOS trial are the main levers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="3. The School carries almost three-quarters of revenue" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    The School, including CapabilityOS, brings in about 72% of Year-3 revenue. The plan's valuation depends on facilitators and CapabilityOS delivering that work instead of the founder. The [facilitator model](/operations/facilitator-model) sits on the critical path.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How we run it

A small human team directs a fleet of AI agents: **3.5 people in Year 1, 11 in Year 2 and 20 in Year 3**, against 5, 15 and 28 in a conventional plan. Agents run the repeatable work (scheduling, support, lead routing, first drafts). People own the moments that build trust: teaching, selling, certifying and hosting. See the [humans + agents operating model](/ai-operations/overview).
