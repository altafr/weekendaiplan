## The business in one paragraph

WeekendAI Labs is a four-brand applied-AI company: an education arm (**School of Applied AI**), an agency (**Studios by WeekendAI**), a hub (**weekendai.com**) and a publishing and community arm (**The AI Edge**). The brands share one audience and feed one super-community. The engine is problem-first AI education for non-technical professionals. The moat is the community. The goal is a business that makes money without depending on the founder, and that a buyer could run without him.

## Three-year targets

| Metric | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $1.8M | $6.2M | **$14.6M** |
| Recurring + contracted share | 20% | 40% | **>55%** |
| Operator Guild members | 600 | 1,800 | 3,800 |
| Cohort graduates (cumulative) | 250 | 850 | 1,833 |
| B2B contracts | 4 | 15 | 30 |
| Facilitator bench | 0 | 3 | 8 |
| Founder-touch revenue | 70% | 45% | **under 30%** |
| EBITDA margin | ~5% | ~22% | ~30% |

*Source: Investor Deck, slide 17 (projection).*

## What the operating model tells us

We rebuilt the revenue path line by line in the [operating model](/financials/three-year-plan). The volumes in the deck do add up to the deck's revenue. The model also shows three things to manage:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1. Recurring share falls short of the 55% sale-readiness gate" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Under base-case assumptions, recurring and contracted revenue reaches about **45%** in Year 3, not 55%. Closing the gap takes roughly $1.5M of revenue moving onto recurring terms. The levers are: multi-year B2B terms, annual Guild billing, and bundling Academy access into the Guild. See [Unit economics](/business-model/unit-economics#closing-the-recurring-gap).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="2. Year-1 CAC runs above target" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Blended CAC is about **$250** in Year 1 against a target below $180. It only reaches target in Years 2–3, once organic and referral traffic from the community carries more of the acquisition load. The free monthly live workshop is the main lever.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="3. The School carries two-thirds of revenue" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    The School brings in about 67% of Year-3 revenue. The plan's valuation depends on facilitators delivering that work instead of the founder. The [facilitator model](/operations/facilitator-model) sits on the critical path.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
