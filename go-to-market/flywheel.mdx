The long-term advantage isn't any single product. It's the community. Every video, lead magnet, newsletter, free framework and course exists to bring people into one engaged network. The community then produces social proof, referrals, content and demand for every paid product across all four brands.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  LM[Lead magnets<br/>frameworks · templates · Scorecard] --> V[Videos & content<br/>YouTube · LinkedIn · newsletters]
  V --> SC((Super-<br/>community))
  SC --> P[Paid products<br/>courses · workshops · agency]
  P --> R[Referrals<br/>social proof · word of mouth]
  R --> N[New audience]
  N --> LM
  W[Free monthly<br/>live workshop] --> SC
  B[Partners & B2B<br/>white-label channels] --> SC
```

*Each turn compounds: community members become advocates, students and buyers across all four brands.*

## Two additions to the loop

<CardGroup cols={2}>
