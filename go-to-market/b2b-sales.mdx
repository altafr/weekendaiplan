B2B contracts are the highest-value, most transferable revenue in the plan. Targets: **4 → 15 → 30 contracts**, contributing about **$2.4M** by Year 3 (model).

## Offer

| Package | Scope | Price band |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate workshop | Full day with a problem set for the client's industry | $10k–14k |
| Team licence | Academy + Guild seats for a team, with an admin dashboard | $25k–50k/yr |
| Enterprise programme | Certification cohorts + Executive Intensive + ROI report | $50k–75k+/yr |

## Sales motion

<Steps>
  <Step title="Source">Leads come from AI Edge readers, Executive Intensive alumni, graduates' employers and partners.</Step>
  <Step title="Land">A corporate workshop is a paid pilot that proves value in one day.</Step>
  <Step title="Prove">Transformation Wall results from the workshop go into an ROI brief for the sponsor.</Step>
  <Step title="Expand">Convert to a team licence or enterprise programme on annual, then multi-year, terms.</Step>
  <Step title="Renew">A quarterly business review with the sponsor, targeting NRR above 110%.</Step>
</Steps>

## Pipeline standards

- Stages: Lead → Discovery → Workshop pilot → Proposal → Closed → Renewal
- Plan-level coverage: pipeline should be 3× the quarterly B2B target
- Every closed deal has a named sponsor, a success metric and a renewal date in the CRM
