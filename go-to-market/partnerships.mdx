WeekendAI Labs is built for collaboration, not isolated growth. Partners can come in at several points: as content collaborators, technology providers, distribution channels or co-trainers.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Education partners" icon="graduation-cap">
    **Co-training & curriculum.** Co-develop and deliver modules within the School. Partners get our cohort infrastructure, marketing and community. *Your IP, our reach.*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tech partners" icon="microchip">
    **Technology & tools.** Put your tools in front of people who are actively building. Sponsored sessions, tool spotlights in newsletters, and inclusion in practical exercises.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Service partners" icon="briefcase">
    **Agency referrals.** Studios gets more leads than it can serve. Overflow referrals, joint pitches and white-label delivery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Media partners" icon="newspaper">
    **Content distribution.** Cross-promotion into The AI Edge community. Newsletter sponsorships, guest articles, joint events and access to decision-makers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Three steps to working together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Exploratory conversation">30 minutes. We share where the brands are and what's gaining traction, and listen to where the partner sees a natural fit.</Step>
  <Step title="Pilot collaboration">One co-created piece of content, a joint event, or an audience swap. Low commitment, high signal.</Step>
  <Step title="Structured partnership">Formalise the arrangement (revenue share, equity, referral or sponsorship) based on what the pilot showed.</Step>
</Steps>

## Good fit if the partner…

- Works with non-technical professionals who need to understand AI
- Has an audience that overlaps with ours
- Builds tools that practitioners actually use
- Wants to reach AI transformation leaders directly
- Sees education as a growth lever, not a cost centre
