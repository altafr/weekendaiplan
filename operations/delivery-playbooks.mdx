## Template: Certification cohort run-sheet

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="T-6 weeks: enrolment">
    Open enrolment, run the free workshop, cap at 12 seats, send the pre-work and the baseline template.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Week 1: Define">
    Kick-off, choose problems, record baselines, form peer pods.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Weeks 2–3: Map & Build">
    Map use cases, run build sprints, hold office hours twice a week.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Weeks 4–5: Build & Measure">
    Prototype review, measurement plan, peer critique.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Week 6: Ship">
    Demo day, certification assessment, Guild onboarding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Week 6 + 2: Proof">
    Collect Wall submissions, verify, publish, and invite graduates to the alumni offer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
