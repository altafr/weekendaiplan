About two-thirds of Year-3 revenue comes from the School. The facilitator bench (**0 → 3 → 8**) decides whether that revenue can transfer to a buyer.

## Recruit → certify → license

<Steps>
  <Step title="Recruit from graduates">The best candidates are Certification graduates with a verified Transformation Wall entry and domain credibility in ops, HR, RevOps or finance.</Step>
  <Step title="Shadow">Co-deliver two cohorts alongside the founder or a lead facilitator.</Step>
  <Step title="Certify">Deliver one cohort solo with observation. Pass criteria: completion of 70% or higher, a satisfaction score of 4.5/5 or higher, and Wall submissions from 60% or more of participants.</Step>
  <Step title="License">Sign a facilitator licence agreement that covers the IP, the curriculum version, the quality standards, and the pay model (salaried bench or per-cohort fee).</Step>
  <Step title="Recertify every year">Recertify annually against the current curriculum version and quality data.</Step>
</Steps>

## Capacity math (model)

| | Y1 | Y2 | Y3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certification seats | 250 | 600 | 983 |
| Cohorts at 12 seats | ~21 | ~50 | ~82 |
| Facilitators | 0 (founder) | 3 | 8 |
| Cohorts per facilitator per year | n/a | ~15 (plus founder) | ~10 |

<Note>Year 1 means about 21 cohorts delivered by the founder alone. That's the tightest capacity constraint in the whole plan. Options: use two facilitators on contract from Q3 of Year 1, or run 12-seat cohorts with a teaching assistant.</Note>

## Quality system

- One master curriculum, version-controlled, with facilitator guides for each session
- A dashboard per cohort: attendance, completion, NPS, Wall submissions
- A quarterly facilitator calibration session
