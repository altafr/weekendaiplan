**Domain:** [studios.weekendai.com](https://studios.weekendai.com) · **Role:** agency and cash generator · **Share of Year-3 revenue:** ~6% (model)

Studios builds AI-powered growth infrastructure for brands. It's faster to deploy and cheaper to iterate than traditional agency work, and it's instrumented from day one. AI tools and methods sit at the core of the work, not added on at the end.

## Role in the portfolio

- **Proof:** every Studios build becomes a case study for the School and The AI Edge.
- **Revenue:** project fees and monthly retainers. Retainers count as recurring revenue.
- **Overflow:** Studios gets more leads than it can serve. Refer extra work to [service partners](/go-to-market/partnerships) or deliver white-label.

<Warning>Keep Studios to about 10% of revenue or less. Agency revenue is valued at a lower multiple than education and community revenue, and it pulls on the founder's time.</Warning>
