**Domain:** [weekendai.com](https://weekendai.com) · **Role:** traffic router, lead capture, community home

The hub doesn't have its own product. It's where people discover every other brand. Visitors arrive from social media, YouTube, newsletters and referrals, and get routed to whichever brand fits what they want.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Audience arrival">
    YouTube · LinkedIn · newsletters · paid ads. All of it goes to weekendai.com first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Intent routing">
    **Learn** → School · **Hire** → Studios · **Read** → The AI Edge · **Join** → Community
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lead capture">
    Free frameworks, templates and the **AI Readiness Scorecard** capture email and profile data before any sale. Community membership is the main call to action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Super-community onboarding">
    Every lead starts a structured community journey. Events, peer groups and content keep people engaged between purchases.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Hub KPIs

| KPI | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Monthly unique visitors, by source | Shows which channels actually bring people |
| Visitor → email capture rate | How well the lead magnets work |
| Routing split (learn / hire / read / join) | Tells us demand by brand |
| Email → first purchase within 60 days | How well the ladder converts |

## Revenue on the hub

Sponsorships and partnerships, plus merchandise and identity drops. Merchandise stays at 6% of revenue or less on purpose.
