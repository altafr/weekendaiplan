**Domain:** [schoolofappliedai.com](https://schoolofappliedai.com) · **Role:** education engine · **Share of Year-3 revenue:** ~67% (model)

Every module starts with a business challenge and works back to the AI solution. The School has no tool tourism and no theory without practice. Students build something real before they leave.

## Delivery formats

| Format | Description | Price point | Delivered by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **On-Demand Courses** | Self-paced; subscription or one-off access; builds toward certification | $79 packs · $149 toolkit · $497 AI Fluency Academy | Recorded once, no live delivery |
| **Cohort Masterclasses** | In person and live online, 6–12 participants, problem-led and hands-on throughout | $2,500 six-week AI Operator Certification | Founder, then facilitators |
| **Executive Intensive** | Leadership programme for senior buyers | $6,000 | Founder (flagship) |
| **Corporate Workshops** | Full day for enterprise teams, with a problem set built for the client's industry | $10k–14k (model) | Facilitators |
| **Certification Track** | A structured path from foundations to practitioner, recognised by enterprises | Bundled | Facilitators + assessors |

## Operating notes

- **Cohort size is capped at 12** to protect completion and outcomes. Scale by adding cohorts and facilitators, not seats.
- Every cohort ends with a **Transformation Wall submission** (see [Transformation proof](/business-model/transformation-proof)).
- The curriculum lives in a version-controlled library so any certified facilitator can deliver it.
