## Three proven models

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Personal-brand AI educators" icon="user-tie">
    These educators teach AI fluency but stay **tied to the founder**. Example: Allie K. Miller runs a free 5-day email course, then a $397 AI-First Academy, then cohorts, then enterprise ([academy.alliekmiller.com](https://academy.alliekmiller.com/)).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Communication-training operators" icon="microphone">
    These operators make money from authority but hit a **founder ceiling** on their premium products. Example: Vinh Giang runs free YouTube, then a free workshop, a ~$499 academy, an A$2,299 live masterclass and keynotes ([vinhgiang.com](https://vinhgiang.com/programs/stage-academy)).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mass-market Indian platforms" icon="users-rectangle">
    These platforms win on volume, not margin, and are **deliberately not for sale**. Example: WebVeda charges ₹1,999/year for all access, has ~5 lakh students and ₹100 Cr in cumulative sales since 2020. It is bootstrapped, and all its distribution runs through the founder ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/webveda/__gE3SvTp2xRssX5gVjp-JMvhpxqFw1T4N3PDV6LYCcsQ) · [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/news/new-updates/who-is-ankur-warikoo-the-finfluencer-behind-webveda-make-epic-money-and-a-rs-100-crore-edtech-business/articleshow/131131813.cms)).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Benchmarks that show the playbook works

- **Maven**: $14M paid to instructors, ~15,000 students, ~$20k average per cohort ([Maven](https://maven.com/maven/course-accelerator))
- **Ali Abdaal, Part-Time YouTuber Academy**: $3.5M generated at $895 ([aliabdaal.com](https://aliabdaal.com/part-time-youtuber-crash-course-ptya-offer/))
- **Sahil Bloom**: 800k+ newsletter subscribers, ~$10M revenue ([Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/starting-a-business/how-sahil-bloom-built-a-newsletter-that-makes-70000-a/467228))
- **Interview Kickstart**: $10M raise, ~20,000 professionals trained ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/davidprosser/2024/02/26/interview-kickstart-raises-10-million-to-scale-up-tech-teaching/))
- **Circle** community platform: $89/mo Professional, $199/mo Business on annual billing ([Circle](https://circle.so/pricing))
