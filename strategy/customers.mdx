## Core segments

| Segment | Who | Primary brand | Entry offer | Expansion path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Non-technical professionals** | Ops, HR, RevOps, marketing, finance managers | School of Applied AI | Free AI Readiness Scorecard, $79 workflow pack | Academy → Guild → Certification |
| **AI transformation leaders** | Heads of transformation, CDOs, strategy directors, ambitious managers | The AI Edge | Bi-weekly newsletter, playbooks | Guild → Executive Intensive → B2B programme |
| **Businesses & brands** | Digital-first SMEs and marketing teams | Studios | Lead-gen website project | Monthly retainer, AI ads, short AI movies |
| **Enterprises** | L&D, HR and transformation functions | School + AI Edge | Corporate workshop | $25k–75k team licence / enterprise programme |
| **Partners** | Education, tech, service and media partners | WeekendAI Hub | Exploratory conversation | Pilot → structured partnership |

## Ideal customer signals

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Individual buyer" icon="user">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Enterprise buyer" icon="building">
    Has an AI adoption target, 20+ non-technical staff to upskill, and a leader who has to report ROI to the board.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
