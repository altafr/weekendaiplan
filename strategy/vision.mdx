## A fourth design principle: built to run without the founder

The investor strategy adds a fourth principle that shapes every operating decision in this plan. **The business must be able to run without the founder.** Buyers ask one diligence question above all others: *"Can we run this without the founder?"*

In practice this means:

- Record the curriculum once, and have certified facilitators deliver it.
- Hold the community on WeekendAI-owned platforms, not on a personal following.
- Keep IP, SOPs and facilitator licensing documented from day one.
- Track founder-touch revenue as a board-level KPI, with the goal of taking it from 70% to under 30%.

## What success looks like in Year 3 (target)

- $14.6M revenue, more than 55% of it recurring or contracted
- 3,800 Operator Guild members and 30 B2B contracts with renewals
- An 8-facilitator bench delivering most School revenue
- The founder at board level, with a GM running operations
