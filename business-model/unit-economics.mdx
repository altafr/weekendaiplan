## Targets vs model

| Metric | Target | Model Y1 | Model Y3 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blended CAC | under $180 | ~$247 | ~$179 | 🟡 Reaches target only by Year 2 |
| LTV (2-yr Guild member, GM basis) | over $1,600 | $1,612 | $1,612 | 🟢 |
| LTV : CAC | over 4:1 | 6.5× | 9.0× | 🟢 |
| Digital-product gross margin | over 85% | 90% | 90% | 🟢 |
| Cohort gross margin | over 70% | 70% | 70% | 🟡 No headroom |
| Merchandise gross margin | 55–65% (apparel norm) | 60% | 60% | 🟢 |
| Guild annual retention | over 80% | Tracked | Tracked | n/a |
| Net revenue retention | over 110% | Tracked | Tracked | n/a |
| Recurring + contracted share | over 55% by Y3 | 31% | **45%** | 🔴 Gap |

*Recurring revenue models get 2–3× higher valuations than one-time models ([ClearlyAcquired](https://www.clearlyacquired.com/blog/how-recurring-revenue-impacts-business-valuation)).*

<Note>CAC in the model = content & paid acquisition spend ÷ new paying customers across the individual ladder. Customers who buy several rungs are counted more than once, so real CAC per unique customer is higher. Track both once the CRM is live.</Note>

## Closing the recurring gap

The model's Year-3 recurring share is about 45%. Reaching 55% means moving about **$1.5M** of Year-3 revenue onto recurring or contracted terms. Options, roughly from easiest to hardest:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Bundle Academy access into the Guild">
    Make the $497 Academy part of an annual Guild tier. If half of Academy revenue moves into the Guild, about $1.2M becomes recurring.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sell cohorts inside B2B contracts">
    Package enterprise cohort seats as annual team licences. Each seat moved from one-off to contracted adds $2,500 to the recurring line.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Multi-year B2B terms">
    Two- or three-year enterprise agreements with annual cohorts built in. This also lifts NRR above 110%.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Executive Intensive alumni circle">
    A paid annual peer circle for Intensive alumni, priced as a premium Guild tier.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
