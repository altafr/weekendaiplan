Every graduate submits a **before/after workflow with a measurable productivity change**. These are combined on a public **Transformation Wall**.

## Why it matters

Cohort-based learning completes at **40–70%**, compared with **5–15%** for self-paced MOOCs ([ProductGrowth.in](https://productgrowth.in/insights/edtech/course-completion-rates-benchmarks/) · [Ruzuku](https://www.ruzuku.com/learn/articles/course-completion-rates)). That completion gap is the outcome proof, and it justifies charging $2,500 instead of $497.

## Submission standard

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| Workflow name and owner role | ✅ |
| Baseline: time, cost, error rate or volume *before* | ✅ |
| AI solution used (tools + prompt/automation summary) | ✅ |
| Result: same metric *after*, measured over at least 2 weeks | ✅ |
| Productivity change (%) and annualised value ($) | ✅ |
| Employer permission to publish (or anonymised) | ✅ |

## Operating process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Baseline in week 1">Each participant records the baseline metric before building anything.</Step>
  <Step title="Build in weeks 2–5">Facilitators coach the build using the Define → Map → Build → Measure → Ship method.</Step>
  <Step title="Measure in week 6 plus 2 weeks">The participant submits the after-metric. The programme manager checks it for plausibility.</Step>
  <Step title="Publish">An approved entry goes on the Wall. Enterprise clients get an aggregate ROI report.</Step>
</Steps>

## KPIs

- Cohort completion rate: **70% or higher** (top of the benchmark range)
- Graduates with a verified Wall submission: **60% or higher**
- Median productivity change, reported quarterly
