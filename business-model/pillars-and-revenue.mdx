## The four core pillars

| Pillar | Revenue streams | Model |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Digital products & services** | On-demand courses (School) · CapabilityOS daily microlearning subscription and core package (School) · Playbook & framework licensing (AI Edge) · Project work: sites, video (Studios) | Subscription/one-off · Per licence · Project fee |
| **2. Transformation** | Cohort masterclasses (School) · Corporate workshops (School) · *Executive Intensives (new)* | Per cohort · Project fee · Ticket |
| **3. Community** | Premium community membership: the Operator Guild (AI Edge) | Annual subscription. **The MRR engine** |
| **4. Merchandise & identity** | Limited drops tied to certification | Direct. Held to 6% of revenue or less on purpose; it builds identity and advocacy and isn't meant as a profit centre |
| *Portfolio* | Sponsorships & partnerships (WeekendAI) · Agency retainers (Studios) | Partnership fee · Monthly retainer |
