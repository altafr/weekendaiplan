Every agent has **one named human owner**. The owner sets its instructions, reviews its work, and answers for its mistakes. An agent without an owner doesn't go live.

## Autonomy levels

| Level | Name | What the agent may do | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** | Draft | Prepares work; a human sends or publishes it | Proposal drafts, newsletter drafts |
| **L2** | Act with review | Acts, and a human reviews a sample or a queue daily | Learner FAQ replies, lead routing |
| **L3** | Autonomous | Acts alone within fixed rules; escalates exceptions | Reminders, invoicing, scheduling |

## Agent specs (template)

Each agent gets a one-page spec in [Delivery playbooks](/operations/delivery-playbooks) before it launches.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What every spec must contain">
    The agent's job in one sentence. Its inputs and the tools it can use. Its autonomy level. What it must escalate, and to whom. The quality bar and how it's checked. A monthly cost cap. The named owner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Example: Concierge">
    **Job:** Answer learner and member questions within 5 minutes, any time of day. **Tools:** community platform, email, course platform (read), payments (read). **Level:** L2. **Escalate:** refunds, complaints, anything about wellbeing or employment, any enterprise client, any question it can't answer from the knowledge base. **Quality:** Head of AI Ops reviews 20 conversations a week; target 95% correct. **Discloses:** it's an AI assistant, with a one-click route to a person.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Example: Coach / TA">
    **Job:** Help learners get unstuck between live sessions and draft feedback on prototypes. **Tools:** curriculum library, learner submissions, cohort chat. **Level:** L2 for Q&A, L1 for feedback. **Escalate:** a learner who is stuck two sessions running, any disagreement with the facilitator's guidance. **Human rule:** no grade, certification decision or written feedback reaches a learner without a facilitator's approval.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
