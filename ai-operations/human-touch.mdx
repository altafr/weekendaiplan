The test for every step: **would a customer feel short-changed if they found out a machine did this?** If yes, a person does it.

## Across the customer journey

| Stage | Agent does | Human does | Why a person |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Discovery** | Scout scores and routes visitors; Content Studio drafts posts | Founder records videos and writes opinion pieces | The founder's voice is the brand's credibility |
| **Free workshop** | Cohort Ops runs registration and reminders; Concierge answers questions | Founder or lead facilitator teaches it live | This is the highest-converting moment; it has to feel personal |
| **Purchase** | Concierge handles checkout questions; Finance issues receipts | Head of AI Ops handles payment problems and refunds | Money problems need judgment and goodwill |
| **Cohort onboarding** | Cohort Ops sends pre-work and baseline templates | Facilitator sends a personal welcome video | First impressions set completion rates |
| **Live sessions** | Coach / TA answers questions between sessions | Facilitator teaches, coaches, and runs demo day | This is what people pay for |
| **Feedback** | Coach / TA drafts prototype feedback | Facilitator edits and approves every note | Feedback must be accurate and kind |
| **Certification** | Proof checks submissions are complete | Facilitator decides pass or fail | A certificate is a promise to employers |
| **Guild membership** | Community Pulse flags quiet members and churn risk | Community lead hosts events and calls at-risk members | People stay for people |
| **Enterprise sale** | Deal Desk researches, preps and drafts | B2B lead runs every call and negotiation | Enterprise buyers buy trust |
| **Renewal** | Deal Desk builds the QBR pack and ROI report | B2B lead and founder run the review with the sponsor | Renewals are relationship decisions |
| **Complaints** | Concierge acknowledges instantly and escalates | A named person replies within 1 business day | Nobody wants a bot to handle their complaint |

## Always human

<Warning>
These never go to an agent, at any level: live teaching · pass/fail decisions · refunds and complaints · pricing and discounts · contracts and negotiations · hiring and facilitator certification · anything published in the founder's name without his review · conversations about someone's job, stress or wellbeing.
</Warning>

## Human touches worth adding on purpose

These cost little time and are the difference people remember:

- A 60-second personal welcome video from the facilitator to each cohort
- A handwritten-style note (digital or physical) to every graduate who publishes a Wall entry
- A founder call with every Executive Intensive participant and every new B2B sponsor
- A quarterly in-person meetup wherever 30+ Guild members cluster
- A real person's name and face on every escalation reply
