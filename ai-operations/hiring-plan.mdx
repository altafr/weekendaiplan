## What every hire needs

Every person on the team works alongside agents daily, so three traits matter more than a CV:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="AI fluency" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Uses AI tools every day, writes clear instructions, and can tell good agent output from bad.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Judgment" icon="scale-balanced">
    Knows when to trust an agent and when to step in. Escalates early.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Human warmth" icon="heart">
    Good with people under pressure: learners who are stuck, clients who are unhappy.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>**Interview test for every role:** "Show us a workflow you automated with AI, and tell us about a time you overruled it." The best candidates are often School of Applied AI graduates.</Tip>

## Hiring sequence

| Order | Role | When | Type | Why this order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Head of AI Operations** | Y1 Q1 | Full-time | Frees the founder from admin on day one; builds and runs the first agents |
| 2 | **Creative & content lead** | Y1 Q1 | Contract → full-time by Q3 | Content is the top of the flywheel; the founder can't edit his own videos |
| 3 | **Lead facilitator** | Y1 Q3 | Contract (0.5 FTE) | Starts shadowing so the founder isn't the only teacher by Year 2 |
| – | Freelancers | Y1 onward | On demand | Bookkeeper, lawyer, video editor, designer, automation freelancer |
| 4 | **B2B & partnerships lead** | Y2 Q1 | Full-time | 15 contracts in Y2 needs a dedicated seller |
| 5 | **General Manager / COO** | Y2 Q1–Q2 | Full-time | Takes over day-to-day so the founder can step back |
| 6 | **AI automation engineer** | Y2 Q2 | Full-time | Agents become core infrastructure and need an owner who codes |
| 7 | **Community lead** | Y2 Q2 | Full-time | Guild grows past 1,000 members |
| 8 | **CapabilityOS product & growth lead** | Y2 Q1 | Full-time | Subscriptions become 14% of revenue and need an owner for retention and growth |
| 9–10 | **Facilitators #2 and #3** | Y2 Q2–Q3 | Salaried or licensed | Deck target: 3-facilitator bench |
| 11+ | Facilitators #4–8, B2B account exec, community associate, content producer, Studios lead | Y3 | Mixed | Scale delivery; founder moves to board level |

## Role profiles

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Head of AI Operations — the first and most important hire">
    **Owns:** Scout, Concierge and Cohort Ops agents; cohort logistics; SOPs; the escalation queue. **Skills:** process design; no-code automation (Make, n8n, Zapier); prompt writing; CRM and course-platform admin; calm with upset customers. **Background:** operations at a bootcamp, edtech or community business, ideally someone who has already automated their own job. **Cost:** ~$90k loaded (planning estimate).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Creative & content lead">
    **Owns:** Content Studio agent, YouTube and LinkedIn output, brand look across four properties, Studios creative until Year 3. **Skills:** video editing, AI video and image tools, short-form storytelling, platform growth, taste. **Background:** creator-economy producer or agency creative. **Cost:** ~$75k loaded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Facilitators">
    **Owns:** live cohorts, feedback approval, certification decisions; supervises Coach / TA and Proof agents. **Skills:** teaching adults; domain credibility in ops, HR, RevOps, marketing or finance; hands-on AI building; coaching non-technical people. **Background:** Certification graduates with a verified Wall entry, then shadow → certify → license (see [Facilitator model](/operations/facilitator-model)). **Cost:** ~$80k loaded, or per-cohort licence fee.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="B2B & partnerships lead">
    **Owns:** enterprise pipeline, partner programme, renewals; supervises Deal Desk. **Skills:** consultative selling to L&D and transformation leaders; proposal writing; account management. **Background:** corporate training, HR-tech or professional services sales. **Cost:** ~$90k loaded plus commission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="General Manager / COO">
    **Owns:** P&L, hiring, operating rhythm, Finance & Admin agent. **Skills:** running a services or edtech P&L; managing a hybrid human + agent team; hiring. **Background:** GM or COO of a small education or professional services business. **Cost:** ~$165k loaded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI automation engineer">
    **Owns:** building, connecting and testing every agent; evaluations; cost control; data security. **Skills:** Python or TypeScript; LLM APIs and agent frameworks; integrations (CRM, payments, community, course platform); writing evals. **Cost:** ~$110k loaded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CapabilityOS product & growth lead">
    **Owns:** CapabilityOS (capos.live): curriculum roadmap, trial-to-paid conversion, streaks and retention, renewals, monthly live Q&A; supervises the CapabilityOS Coach agent. **Skills:** subscription product management, lifecycle and messaging (Telegram, chat, email), learning design, analytics. **Background:** growth or product at a consumer learning app. **Cost:** ~$100k loaded. **Year 1:** the Head of AI Operations covers this role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Community lead">
    **Owns:** Guild experience, events, retention; supervises Community Pulse. **Skills:** hosting, moderation, programming events, retention analytics. **Cost:** ~$60k loaded.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Where to find people

- **School of Applied AI graduates:** they already know the method and the tools
- **The Hong Kong GenAI community and wider network** for early operators and facilitators
- **Facilitators across time zones:** Asia, the Middle East and Europe let cohorts run around the clock
- **Freelance marketplaces** for editors, designers and bookkeepers before making full-time hires
